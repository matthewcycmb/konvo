## 1. Vancouver Tech Journal

**To:** william@vantechjournal.com

**Subject:** I'm 16 and grew my first app to 10,000+ downloads in 28 days

```text
Hi William,

I'm Matthew, a 16-year-old high school student in Vancouver, and I built Konvo, an app that lets people use Instagram messages without the feed, Reels, or Explore.

This is my first mobile app, and within 28 days of launch I grew it organically to 10,000+ downloads and hit my first $10k month.

I first launched on LinkedIn, where my post reached 220,000+ impressions in 3 days. Then I started promoting it through Instagram trial reels and TikTok. One reel explaining my age and the app got 2M views in less than 24 hours.

Would you be interested in sharing my story on Vancouver Tech Journal? I'd be happy to talk about how I built and grew Konvo while still in high school.

My full story: https://www.starterstory.com/stories/konvoinstall-com
The reel: https://www.instagram.com/p/Dd0TQrPo5mZ/

Thanks,
Matthew Chan
```
