## If you already sent the earlier pitch

```text
I'd love to do an on-camera or recorded interview about how I built and grew Konvo. Happy to give a short demo too.
```

For radio, replace “on-camera or recorded” with “radio”.

## Make it easy to book you

- Keep the reel link in the initial email so producers can see you explaining the app on camera.
- Add a phone number and a couple of genuine availability windows in Pacific time if you want to make scheduling faster. These are optional additions, not missing placeholders in the drafts.
- Have a short screen recording of Konvo and dated download/revenue screenshots ready for anyone who replies. Use a demo conversation with no private messages visible.
- Offer a Vancouver interview or a remote recording, whichever you can actually do.
- LinkedIn is optional in your signature; the reel is the more useful first link for this goal.
- TV/radio newsrooms decide how a story is produced and distributed. Request the interview format explicitly; an email to a broadcaster alone does not guarantee an on-air appearance.

Send each approach individually. Follow up once after about five business days. Keep Pat's existing thread if you already contacted him.
