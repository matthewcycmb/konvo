# App Store featuring nomination

## Description

I'm Matthew, a 16-year-old high school student from Vancouver, and I built Konvo, an app that lets people keep their Instagram messages without the feed, Reels, or Explore.

This is my first mobile app, and I spent almost my entire summer learning how to build and launch it. Within 28 days of launch, I grew Konvo organically to 10,000+ downloads and hit my first $10k month. I started by sharing it on LinkedIn, then posting Instagram trial reels and TikToks. One simple video explaining my age and the app got 2M views in less than 24 hours.

I built Konvo because I wanted to quit scrolling without losing touch with my friends. I'd love to share the story of building my first app at 16 and helping other people stay connected without getting pulled back into scrolling.

## Helpful Details

I tried to quit scrolling for 2 years. When I deleted Instagram for 2 weeks, I came back to over 15 unread messages from my friends. That's why I built Konvo. People can keep their Instagram conversations without asking their friends to download another app. I'm building and growing it on my own while still in high school.
