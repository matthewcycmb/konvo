Start with CTV Vancouver, CBC Vancouver, and UpFlip. Then try Greg Isenberg and Brett Malinowski for a recorded app-building/growth conversation. My First Million and Foundr are stretch approaches. ## Audience context

- UpFlip advertises **1.7M+ YouTube subscribers** on its [own site](https://accelerator.upflip.com/membership-v3). It emphasizes service businesses, so Konvo is a less typical subject even though the interview format fits.
- Brett's [official site](https://www.thebrettway.com/) states **500K+ YouTube subscribers**, labeled as of August 2026.
- CNBC Make It describes reaching millions of readers and viewers monthly on its [About page](https://www.cnbc.com/about-make-it/). That is a combined audience claim, not the audience of any single video.
- Greg and My First Million have active YouTube/podcast operations linked from their official sites. I have not added unverified current subscriber counts.
- Foundr claims a multi-million-person community on its application page. That is not a YouTube subscriber count.

These figures are the publishers' own claims and do not predict the views your interview would receive.

## Applications

### 9. CNBC Make It — Millennial Money

**Apply:** [Casting form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeJm55q1SQEsX2E3CX1I4YKINtD91rGNmGiUhG-I24rOVSBXw/viewform).

The form invites Gen Z through baby boomers to apply, asks for location and income, and asks whether you are comfortable providing financial evidence. It does not explicitly confirm eligibility for a 16-year-old Canadian; be clear about both your age and location.

Use this for **“What's the most interesting thing about you?”**:

```text
I'm a 16-year-old high school student in Vancouver, and I built Konvo, my first mobile app. It lets people use Instagram messages without the feed, Reels, or Explore.

Within 28 days of launch, I grew it organically to 10,000+ downloads and hit my first $10k month. I promoted it through LinkedIn, Instagram trial reels, and TikTok, and one reel got 2M views in less than 24 hours.

I'd love to share what it's been like building and growing my first app while still in high school.

My story: https://www.starterstory.com/stories/konvoinstall-com
```

For **the short intro video**, your reel is an available starting point: https://www.instagram.com/p/Dd0TQrPo5mZ/

Fill the personal background and money questions with your actual answers. The form asks for annual salary: app revenue is not personal salary, and one $10k month should not be presented as $120k of annual earnings.

CNBC also publishes `askmakeit@cnbc.com` for [career and personal-finance questions](https://cnbc.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/15459050355611-How-do-I-contact-the-Make-It-team). I have not treated it as a verified video-booking inbox.
