Create exactly FIVE different fictional adults as tight face portraits inside five equal circular avatars in a single horizontal row. Diverse natural appearances: an East Asian man, a Black woman, a white man, a South Asian woman, a Latino man, all approximately 25–35, friendly subtle smiles, realistic skin and casual everyday appearance. Entire asset is just this compact row, no text, numbers, ratings, logos, stars, or claims of endorsement. Plain white background, tightly fitted canvas around the avatar row with minimal margins, wide horizontal composition. These are invented illustrative people, not real customers.
