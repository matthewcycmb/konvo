Recommended test & production notes

01 / Start with Electric

The first frame immediately names Instagram and the DMs-only value. It preserves the existing blue identity, with a larger product hero and fewer words. This is the strongest clarity-first hypothesis.

02 / Test against the current set

Use Apple Product Page Optimization to compare a treatment with the original. Keep icon, subtitle, locale, and traffic conditions consistent. At low volume, test one challenger at a time; wait for sufficient data rather than declaring a winner after a fixed number of days.

03 / Refine the winning direction

These sets change both copy and art direction, so a result identifies the stronger package. Next, isolate the first headline or hero visual. Use Apple’s conversion results and confidence estimates, with paid conversion and retention as downstream checks.

Asset note: the exported headlines and graphics are rendered at full resolution.
