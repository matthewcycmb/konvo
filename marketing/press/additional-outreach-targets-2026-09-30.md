## Today's approach

Aim for roughly 15–25 relevant approaches across the original outlets, Starter Story, and this list. That is a suggested workload, not a limit. Build a larger list over time and use replies to improve the next batch.

Keep the news emails similar, as requested. Change the recipient and closing sentence. For companies, use the customer-story messages above so the connection to their product is clear.

Record the outlet, person, date, message, and result. Start with one person or newsroom per outlet and follow up once after about five business days. Count a form submission as an approach, too.

The goal is interviews, coverage, and relevant readers. A target of 100 sends is only useful if each recipient has a reason to care about Konvo.
