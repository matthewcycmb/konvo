# CNBC Make It application

## Tell us about your background, and how you grew up relating to money.

I grew up in Hong Kong and moved to Vancouver when I was 12. My family was financially stable, so money wasn't a big problem for us growing up.

When I was 12 and in Grade 7, I created a Roblox store where I sold virtual accessories. I started because I was bored and wanted to see if I could make Robux online. I remember coming home from school and immediately working on modelling and uploading items. I ended up making 500,000 Robux from it.

I'm ambitious and like taking action. I try to focus on what I can control and do as much as I can with it.

## What's been your biggest challenge in life so far?

Building and growing Konvo was a big challenge because it was my first mobile app. I spent almost my entire summer learning how to build and launch it. After launching, I was getting maybe 20 or 30 downloads a day and started feeling like I was plateauing and doubting myself. Then my parents encouraged me to make a simple video explaining my age and the app. That video got 2M views in less than 24 hours.

## What's the biggest money mistake you've made?

I haven't made any major money mistakes yet. I'm still a high school student and am learning as I go.

## What do you do for fun?

I love playing tennis and pickleball, and watching K-dramas with my mom. I also enjoy watching TV shows and movies. Apart from that, most of my free time goes into building and growing Konvo.

## What's the most interesting thing about you?

I'm a 16-year-old high school student in Vancouver, and I built Konvo, my first mobile app. It lets people use Instagram messages without the feed, Reels, or Explore.

Within 28 days of launch, I grew it organically to 10,000+ downloads and hit my first $10k month. I promoted it through LinkedIn, Instagram trial reels, and TikTok, and one reel got 2M views in less than 24 hours.

I'd love to share what it's been like building and growing my first app while still in high school.

My story: https://www.starterstory.com/stories/konvoinstall-com

## What are your big picture goals?

Right now, I want to keep working on Konvo and grow it as big as I can. My goal is to help a million, or even 10 million people worldwide reduce their screen time and stop getting stuck scrolling.

Even if I'm not working on Konvo in the future, I still want to build businesses. I'm very product-oriented. I love making products, promoting them, and seeing people actually use them.
